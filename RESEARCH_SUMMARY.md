## STRATEGIC PATTERNS FOR ENTREPRENEURS

### 1. Vertical SaaS Dominance ✓
Examples: Harvey (legal), PathAI (pathology), Qure.ai (radiology)
- Target: High error cost + regulatory moat
- Pricing: Premium for domain-specific accuracy
- Distribution: Doctor/lawyer adoption > IT buyer

### 2. Agentification of Every Role ✓
Examples: Ergo (sales), Augment (logistics), Momentic (testing)
- Winner: First agentic assistant in each vertical with proven ROI
- Positioning: Task completion > task assistance
- Pricing: Outcomes-based (% deflection, % approval, time saved)

### 3. Infrastructure Arbitrage ✓
Examples: Runware (model aggregation), Infinigence (chip orchestration)
- Play: Cost optimization + vendor flexibility
- Moat: Network effects (more models = more valuable)
- Revenue: Consumption-based + premium for optimization

### 4. Open-Source as Moat ✓
Examples: Mistral, Hugging Face, Dify
- Model: Open-source core + commercial licensing
- TAM: Developer adoption → enterprise upsell
- Adoption: Faster than closed-source alternatives

### 5. Pricing Power Through Outcomes ✓
Examples: Ada (deflection %), Gini (approval %), Upstart (credit)
- Model: Outcomes-based guarantees
- Advantage: Uncapped upside; customer success = business success
- Risk: Requires proof of impact (pilot)

### 6. Founder Pedigree = Capital Magnet ✓
Examples: Poolside (GitHub founder), Ankar (Palantir vets), Mind Robotics (Rivian spin-out)
- Signal: Founder credibility attracts capital + talent
- Advantage: Fast hiring + customer introductions
- Risk: Founder-dependent; retention key

### 7. Non-Consensus Markets ✓
Examples: DeepSeek (Chinese models), Infinigence (chip orchestration), Sunday (home robots)
- Advantage: Less crowded; differentiation through consensus-breaking
- Risk: Market may not be ready
- Signal: First mover in new category

### 8. Creator/SMB Distribution ✓
Examples: Lovable (dev tools), Base44 (no-code), Tripo (3D)
- Model: Free/freemium → paid pro
- Advantage: Viral potential > B2B sales
- Metric: User growth > revenue (early stage)

### 9. Founder-Led Sales ✓
Examples: Early-stage Series A founders closing large enterprise deals
- Signal: Founder = closing power
- Advantage: Product credibility; founder as CMO
- Risk: Doesn't scale past Series C

### 10. Distance to Money ✓
Examples: Trading (closest) > CRM > Analytics > Infrastructure (farthest)
- Principle: Closer to revenue = higher willingness to pay
- Metric: Enterprise ARR growth rates
- Signal: Reduce time to revenue > feature completeness
