## 20. NON-OBVIOUS OPPORTUNITIES & STRATEGIES

### Key Patterns Identified:

1. **Vertical SaaS Dominance**: Harvey, PathAI, Qure.ai, Sandboxing success metrics. Target domains with:
   - High error cost (law, medicine, finance)
   - Regulatory moat (compliance)
   - Doctor/lawyer adoption over IT buyer

2. **Agentification of Every Role**: From sales (Ergo) to logistics (Augment) to testing (Momentic). Winner = first agentic assistant in each vertical that justifies ROI.

3. **Infrastructure Arbitrage**: Model aggregation (Runware), chip orchestration (Infinigence), cost optimization (LiteLLM). Infrastructure layers = recurring revenue.

4. **Open-Source as Moat**: Hugging Face, Mistral, Dify. Community + commercial licensing = faster adoption + enterprise revenue.

5. **Pricing Power Through Outcomes**: Ada (deflection %), Gini (approval %), Upstart (credit access). Outcomes-based = uncapped upside.

6. **Founder Pedigree**: Poolside (GitHub), Ankar (Palantir), Decart (serial founders). Recognition = capital + talent + credibility.

7. **Non-consensus Markets**: Chinese models (DeepSeek), heterogeneous chips (Infinigence), household robots (Sunday). Consensus = crowded.

8. **Creator/SMB Distribution**: Lovable, Base44, Tripo. Permissionless tools = viral adoption > B2B sales.

9. **Founder-Led Sales**: Early-stage Series A founders selling to large enterprises. Founder = closing power.

10. **Distance to Money**: Trading/payments > CRM > analytics > infrastructure. Closer to revenue = higher willingness to pay.
